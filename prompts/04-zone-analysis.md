# Step 4 – Affected city and zone, plus a red herring

**Goal:** find where the refund increase is concentrated.

## Prompt

```text
Compare refunds in August vs October 2026 by city, by zone and by refund reason.
For each zone show the increase in ₹ and its share of the total increase.
Identify the top contributing city and zone and the reasons driving it.

Then check the red herring: Cloud Kitchens Hub restaurants launched on 1 Sep 2026.
Compare their refund counts, refund amounts and missing-item refunds with Standard
restaurants, and estimate how much of the total increase they explain.
Conclude based on the data whether they are the main cause.
```

## Checkpoint

- **City:** Bengaluru.
- **Zone:** Whitefield-East, about ₹1.6 lakh of the ₹2.05 lakh increase (about 78%).
- **Reasons:** Late delivery and Cold food.
- **Cloud Kitchens Hub:** a higher missing-item rate, but a small contribution in ₹ (a few thousand), so not the main cause.
