# Step 3 – Monthly refund analysis

**Goal:** confirm the COO's "refunds up 40%" claim.

## Prompt

```text
For each month of 2026, calculate:
order count, GMV (sum of ORDER_VALUE_INR), refund amount, refund amount as % of GMV,
month-over-month refund growth and order growth.
Attribute each refund to the month the order was placed.

Chart the monthly refunds, GMV, orders and refund % of GMV.
Then compare August with October 2026 explicitly.
```

## Checkpoint

| Metric | Aug 2026 | Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Refunds | ₹5.08 lakh | ₹7.13 lakh (+40%) |
| Refund % of GMV | 3.71% | 4.76% |
| Orders | 27,418 | 30,016 (+9%) |
